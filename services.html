The menu

Services

Four things, done properly. Every service starts with a consultation and ends with a finish you'd walk out of a magazine shoot in.

01

Skin fades

Zero-to-blend precision — tight on the sides, seamless through the top. Low, mid or high, finished with foil detailing and a razor-sharp hairline. The cut the shop's name is built on.

02

Beard sculpting

Shape, line-up and detail work that turns a beard into a statement. Hot-lather edging, neckline carved clean, finished with balm and a comb-through lesson.

03

Hot-towel shaves

The old-school ritual: steamed towel to soften, rich lather, straight razor in steady hands, cold-towel finish. Slow, sharp and worth every minute.

04

Kids cuts

Patient, friendly first-chair experiences for young gents. Sharp looks without the fuss — parents welcome to stay and watch.

The ritual

Every chair, the same standard

Consult. We talk it through — what you want, what suits your head shape, what your last barber got wrong.

Cut. Clippers, scissors, razor — the right tool for every section. No rushing, no conveyor belt.

Finish. Hot-lather edges, styling product worked in properly, and an honest word on how to keep it looking this good at home.
