02 — The Skullfades Foundation

1,200 free haircuts. Zero charge. All heart.

Since opening its doors in 2014, Skull Fades has run the Skullfades Foundation — giving free haircuts to homeless people across Manchester. In 2017 the shop's 1,200th free haircut was featured in Messenger Newspapers, with the work backed by the Manchester mayor.

A cut is dignity. A fresh fade before a job interview, a shave before a fresh start — the Foundation has been doing that quietly, week after week, for over a decade.
